## Context

What is true that forces a decision? Constraints, not preferences.

## Decision

What we are doing, in one or two sentences.

## Consequences

What this costs us, what it buys us, and what it makes hard later. Be honest
about the downside — a record with no downside listed is a record nobody
thought about.
